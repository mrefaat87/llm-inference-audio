Jenny: Extra compute on the decode path. You have to decompress — project the latent vector back up to reconstruct the full keys and values when you actually need them. Alex: Beautiful. Now let me push you on something. You said your target workload is agentic with long outputs — a thousand tokens or more. What architectural choice addresses the decode throughput problem specifically?

Jenny: Multi-token prediction. And the key insight is that it's not a bolt-on optimization. It starts during training. Jenny: Because a separate draft model is like a caching layer with different consistency semantics than your source of truth. It was trained independently. Jenny: About 1.8x decode throughput. Instead of one token per forward pass, you're averaging closer to two. And — here's the connection to the workload — that speedup amortizes over long outputs.
